### Instructions: Make Me Breakfast

1. Go to the kitchen and carry two eggs in the refrigerator.
2. Take out the eggs and place them on the kitchen counter.
3. Get a small bowl and a fork.
4. Crack both eggs into the bowl.
5. Use the fork to beat the eggs until the yolks and whites are mixed.
6. Get a frying pan and place it on the cooker.
7. Turn on the cooker and set it to medium heat.
8. Add one teaspoon of cooking oil to the frying pan.
9. Pour the beaten eggs into the frying pan.
10. Cover the pan and allow the eggs to cook for five minutes.
11. Turn off the cooker after five minutes.
12. Get a clean plate and a spatula.
13. Use the spatula to move the cooked eggs from the pan onto the plate.
14. Get one glass and pour juice into it.
15. Carry the plate and glass of juice to the dining table.
16. Place the plate on the table and put the glass of juice beside it.
17. The task is complete when the eggs and juice are ready on the dining table.

### Objective — Tier 1

My understanding:

Computers require precise instructions because they cannot think or make assumptions like humans.

Humans can use their experience and common sense to understand incomplete instructions. However, computers need us to provide all the necessary information and clear steps to complete a task correctly.

Example:

If I tell a human, "Make me breakfast," they might understand what I mean.

But if I tell a computer the same thing, I must explain what food to prepare, what ingredients to use, how to cook it and how to serve it.

Key takeaway:

A computer can only follow the instructions and information provided to it. Clear instructions help it perform tasks correctly.
